# Character art for the Dirac site

Drop PNG files into this folder. The site picks them up by filename; until a file exists, a quiet placeholder with that filename is shown in its place.

## Files

| File | Where | Pose |
|---|---|---|
| `pet-cat.png` | Playground → Quantum Pet | Idle, front 3/4 view, floating, centred |
| `fighter-hadamard.png` | Playground → Qubit Arena, left side | Idle guard stance, facing right |
| `fighter-hadamard-attack.png` | Arena, left side, on hit | Mid-strike, facing right (optional, enables the hit animation) |
| `fighter-pauli.png` | Playground → Qubit Arena, right side | Idle guard stance, facing right (the site mirrors it) |
| `fighter-pauli-attack.png` | Arena, right side, on hit | Mid-strike, facing right (optional) |

## Specs

- PNG with a fully transparent background, 2048 × 2048, the figure fills about 85% of the height, feet near the bottom edge.
- Same camera and scale for every figure, so they line up when placed side by side.
- Dark-friendly: no white backgrounds, no ground shadow, no text, no watermark, no frame.
- Palette: near-black body, warm off-white (#f2f1ec) highlights, one cold blue accent (#4d8bff) for energy and edges. No purple, no neon rainbow.
- Style: sculpted, physical characters, like collectible figures rendered with soft studio light. Surfaces half solid, half translucent wireframe, as if the figure is in superposition.

## Character notes

- **Hadamard** (left fighter): tall, symmetrical, split down the middle: one half solid matte black, the other half a glowing lattice. Weapon or stance built around a mirror or a plane.
- **Pauli** (right fighter): compact, heavy, with a flip motif: the body looks mirrored or inverted (an X gate flips a qubit). Gloves or fists, grounded stance.
- **Pet, day one**: a cat made of a lattice of glowing points and thin lines, sitting, tail curled, eyes as two bright points. Should read as "cat" at thumbnail size.
